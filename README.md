# Hey, I'm Luca 👋

I build the algorithms that tell robots where they are and what's around them.

**Assistant Professor at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/)**, where I co-lead the [Robot Vision & Perception (RVP) group](https://www.rvp-group.net) together with Prof. Giorgio Grisetti.

My research focuses on **SLAM**, **3D reconstruction**, and **active perception** — combining model-based optimization with learning-driven techniques for robust multimodal perception. Recent work explores Gaussian scene representations, continuous-time SLAM, and GPU-accelerated 3D mapping.

### 🔬 Research interests
`SLAM` · `3D Reconstruction` · `Active Perception` · `Multimodal Calibration` · `Gaussian Splatting` · `Continuous-time SLAM`
